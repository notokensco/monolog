3. Software Overview

Description of service.

Monolog combines everyday typing, natural voice dictation (Vox mode), contextual intelligence, and a private personal journal into a single unified mobile utility. Features include:

  • The Keyboard (IME): QWERTY keyboard with borderless keys, Paper and Ink visual themes, personalized suggestion strips, and clipboard management.
  • Vox Voice Typing: Integrated microphone dictation featuring real-time audio waveform processing, formatting refinement, and punctuation cleanup.
  • Floating AI Assistant: On-demand assistant summoned via the lightning spacebar for drafting assistance, rewriting, and cited web queries.
  • The Context Engine (`m` Key): Screen capture, voice note recording, and quick shortcut creation (reminders, alarms, journal entries).
  • The Companion Journal: Daily summaries, reverse-chronological memory timelines, monthly narrative recaps, and semantic search.

5. Assistive Intelligence Boundaries

Suggestions, evidence & reality.

Monolog incorporates artificial intelligence models—both on-device (Google Gemma 2B via LiteRT-LM) and cloud-grounded (Firebase AI Logic + Gemini 1.5 Flash). You acknowledge and agree to the following operational boundaries:

Separation of Evidence from Fact

Typing a sentence such as “I will pay the rent tonight” or “exam at 5 PM” is evidence that you drafted those words. It does not prove that rent was paid, an exam was confirmed, or a message was delivered. Monolog represents only your available side of communication.

  • AI Suggestions Require Review: Proactive inline suggestion pills (such as reminder pills or suggested alarms) are automated recommendations. Monolog will never automatically commit an event, schedule a reminder, or send a draft without your explicit tap, verification, and confirmation.
  • Probabilistic Nature: AI models generate responses based on probabilistic pattern matching. While designed to be helpful, answers generated by the Floating AI panel, automated tone rewrites, and monthly recaps may occasionally contain inaccuracies or incomplete context. You are solely responsible for reviewing any AI-generated text before using or inserting it into third-party applications.
  • No Duty of Verification: Monolog does not verify external real-world commitments, calendar conflicts, or factual claims made in host applications.

8. Android Platform & Privileges

Device permissions & system integrations.

Monolog operates on Android utilizing standard platform permissions:

  • Input Method Service (IME): Required to function as your system keyboard. Android displays a standard security warning when any IME is enabled. Monolog respects this privilege through strict local encryption and zero raw keystroke logging.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively during active Vox voice dictation or voice note recording. Audio is never recorded passively in the background.
  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection): Used exclusively when you tap the m key and explicitly confirm screen capture. Monolog honors FLAG_SECURE and cannot capture protected banking or DRM screens.
  • Storage & Backup: Used for storing encrypted Room databases and cached context images in private app sandbox storage.

You may revoke optional permissions (such as microphone or screen capture) at any time through Android Settings without forfeiting your ability to use Monolog as a standard keyboard.
