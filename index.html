How does Monolog differ from its competitors?+

Monolog connects your keyboard, voice input, AI assistance and personal journal in one experience. It helps you communicate and act in the app you're already using, then revisit the thoughts and context you choose to keep — your side of the day.

What gets added to my journal?+

Memory capture requires your consent. Eligible, non-sensitive input and the notes, screenshots or voice notes you choose to save can contribute to your journal. You can review, edit and delete entries. A typed draft does not prove that a message was sent or an event happened.

Does it read other people's messages?+

The keyboard works with your input. It doesn't automatically read incoming conversations. You can choose to capture screen context with Android's permission, subject to the app's restrictions.

Can I use it offline?+

Everyday typing, local journals and basic search are designed to work offline. Voice recognition and local AI depend on device support. Explicit cloud AI requests and optional backup require an internet connection.

When can I get Monolog?+

Monolog is in development. This website is an interactive product preview; the keyboard, journal and AI features shown describe the intended product. Email monolog@notokens.co to ask about Android release updates.
