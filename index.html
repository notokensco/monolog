The capture center on your phone

Context Library.
Saved moments, ready when you need them.

Screenshots, camera rolls, bookmarks, and voice memos usually end up scattered across half a dozen apps. The Context Library brings them together into one calm, searchable space without making you leave what you're doing.

One-Tap Capture from Anywhere

Tap or hold the m key in the demo for Reminder, Alarm, Journal, and Save a note. With permission, Monolog can also save selected screen context and a note.

On-Device OCR & Visual Search

Screenshots are never dead images. Local ML Kit OCR automatically extracts readable text from tickets, timetables, slides, and receipts so you can find them by what was written on them.

Spoken Thoughts with Live Waveforms

Speak passing thoughts on the commute home. Monolog saves the audio with responsive waveforms, provides an accurate transcript, and preserves your natural phrasing.

Connected to Today's Journal

Saved context doesn't sit in a disconnected silo. Every item you save automatically threads into that day's journal timeline, giving your memories genuine anchor points.

Ask us something else
How does Monolog differ from its competitors?+

Monolog connects your keyboard, voice input, AI assistance and personal journal in one experience. It helps you communicate and act in the app you're already using, then revisit the thoughts and context you choose to keep — your side of the day.

What gets added to my journal?+

Memory capture requires your consent. Eligible, non-sensitive input and the notes, screenshots or voice notes you choose to save can contribute to your journal. You can review, edit and delete entries. A typed draft does not prove that a message was sent or an event happened.

Does it read other people's messages?+

Monolog’s keyboard works with the text you type or dictate and has no direct access to incoming messages. With Android’s permission, you can separately choose to save screen context, subject to the app’s restrictions.

Can I use it offline?+

Everyday typing, local journals and basic search are designed to work offline. Voice recognition and local AI depend on device support. Web results, downloads and optional backup require an internet connection.

When can I get Monolog?+

Monolog is in development. This website is an interactive product preview; the keyboard, journal and AI features shown describe the intended product. Join the waitlist for Android launch updates.
