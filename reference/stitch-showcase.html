Technical Architecture Specifications

Android IME System Service & Google Gemma Integration

Android 12 (API 31)+ Jetpack Compose IME

1. Local Storage Layer

Encrypted SQLite Room database keyed with Android Keystore 256-bit AES-GCM. Keys never leave the secure hardware enclave (TEE/StrongBox).

2. On-Device Gemma SLM

Google Gemma 2B model executed via LiteRT-LM (TensorFlow Lite runtime) directly on Snapdragon/Tensor NPUs for offline summarization and formatting.

3. Optional Cloud Relay

Firebase AI Logic + Gemini 1.5 Flash utilized strictly when user triggers the web search pill in the floating panel. Explicit opt-in per query.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Monolog a keylogger?

No. Traditional keyloggers silently capture every raw keystroke and exfiltrate them to remote servers. Monolog runs on-device, never tracks raw character streams, and automatically pauses on passwords and banking interfaces. You have granular control to pause memory for 15 minutes or wipe entries at any time.

Does Monolog read other people's incoming messages?

No. As an input method editor (IME), Monolog only interacts with what you type or dictate. It does not inspect other participants' chats unless you explicitly tap the 'm' key to take a screen context snapshot with your Android permission. Hence our motto: Remember your side of the day.

Can I use Monolog completely offline without an internet connection?

Yes. The core QWERTY keyboard, local SQLite Room storage, Vox speech model, and Gemma 2B on-device models operate 100% offline. Network connectivity is only utilized if you choose to trigger cloud web-grounding or Google Drive encrypted backups.

How does Monolog differ from Gboard or SwiftKey?

Standard keyboards treat text as ephemeral: once sent, the context vanishes. Monolog bridges your keyboard with a personal ambient journal. It automatically aggregates your commitments, daily feelings, and screen notes into a peaceful personal timeline without you needing to manually maintain a diary app.
